YouTube Connection, Live Broadcast & Removal Flow

September 22, 2026
1

Coach signs in

The adult coach signs into StecStats and opens the Profile or game-recording screen.

→
2

Selects Connect YouTube

The coach explicitly starts the connection flow. StecStats redirects to Google OAuth.

→
3

Reviews Google consent

Google displays the requested YouTube permissions. The coach chooses whether to grant access.

→
4

Returns connected

StecStats securely stores the encrypted authorization token and displays connection status.

↓
8

Disconnects or deletes

The coach can revoke access from Profile or delete the account, removing stored authorization and connection data.

7

Broadcast completes

When the coach ends the session, StecStats transitions the YouTube Live broadcast to complete.

←
6

YouTube Live runs

StecStats creates and binds the broadcast and stream. Status checks support recovery and completion.

←
5

Coach starts a game

The coach chooses YouTube Live, confirms the title and privacy, and explicitly starts broadcasting.

←
Optional video upload: A coach may separately choose a saved game or highlight and initiate an upload to their YouTube account. User control: No broadcast or upload is created without a coach action. Access can be revoked at any time.
