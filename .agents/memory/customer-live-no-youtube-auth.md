---
name: Customer Live without YouTube setup
description: Product constraint for coach-facing Live and YouTube account ownership.
---

Customers must not be required to connect a Google/YouTube account to record or share a live game. Keep customer games off YouTube entirely, including any dedicated platform channel; the user explicitly rejected YouTube as a destination for customer video. StecStats Live currently uses per-game Daily rooms and watch links, but a link alone is not a privacy control. A connected YouTube channel is only for the owner's own workflow, never a customer prerequisite.

The user wants both live viewing and post-game film review, but expects the subscription to be around $20/month. Do not assume unlimited participant-minute streaming and cloud recording are viable at that price. Any customer-facing Live design needs bounded unit economics at several games per month with multiple viewers; recording/review must remain possible without an expensive Live session.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected customer-managed YouTube setup as too much hassle, and later rejected a platform YouTube channel because coaches may object to team video being easily accessible. Requiring YouTube for Live contradicted the intended product experience. Metered cloud Live and recording can consume most or all of a low monthly subscription in only a few games.

**How to apply:** Treat YouTube relay errors as separate from Daily Live/recording errors. Never tell customers to connect a channel or route customer games to YouTube. Do not call a possession-of-link watch page private or access-controlled. Preserve Live and film review as product goals, but validate privacy and per-game costs before committing to a delivery architecture or pricing. Test simultaneous streams separately before promising scale.
