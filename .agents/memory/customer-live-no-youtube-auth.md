---
name: Customer Live without YouTube setup
description: Product constraint for coach-facing Live and YouTube account ownership.
---

Customers must not be required to connect a Google/YouTube account to record or share a live game. Keep customer games off YouTube entirely, including any dedicated platform channel; the user explicitly rejected YouTube as a destination for customer video. StecStats Live currently uses per-game Daily rooms and watch links, but a link alone is not a privacy control. A connected YouTube channel is only for the owner's own workflow, never a customer prerequisite.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected customer-managed YouTube setup as too much hassle, and later rejected a platform YouTube channel because coaches may object to team video being easily accessible. Requiring YouTube for Live contradicted the intended product experience.

**How to apply:** Treat YouTube relay errors as separate from Daily Live/recording errors. Never tell customers to connect a channel or route customer games to YouTube. Do not call a possession-of-link watch page private or access-controlled; ask whether live video is still wanted before designing viewer authorization. Test simultaneous streams separately before promising scale.
