---
name: iPad recording background sharing
description: Why live-link sharing must happen before starting an iPad recording
---

Do not open the iOS share sheet or Messages while an iPad camera recording is active. Start Go Live and share its link before the coach starts the game clock and recording.

**Why:** Switching to Messages backgrounds StecStats. iPadOS suspends the active camera preview/recording session, leaving the returned recording view frozen or unreliable.

**How to apply:** Block system sharing while the recorder is active and explain the required order. Keep the watch address and session code visible/selectable in-app. Do not imply that an app can continue ordinary CameraView recording while backgrounded.
