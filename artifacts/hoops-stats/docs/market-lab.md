# StecStats market lab

Status: development concept, not an approved campaign or a published offer.

## Product boundary

- **Coach's installed iPad app:** in-game scoring and camera controls.
- **Web/PWA:** discovery, account access, season and player context, and viewing shared game media. A PWA is not assumed to make long camera recordings or live streaming more dependable on iPad.
- **Customer onboarding:** do not require a YouTube connection. A shareable watch link is not access control; describe it accurately.

## Message hypotheses to test

1. **Coach:** “Keep the scorebook, player progress, and game moments together.” Test whether this is clearer than leading with streaming.
2. **Parent/family:** “Follow an athlete across teams and seasons.” Test whether the season story brings families back after one game.
3. **Team organizer:** “Give families one place to follow games and player updates.” Validate actual team permissions before making privacy claims.

These are hypotheses, not proven demand. Avoid testimonials, usage numbers, guaranteed recording, or “private” claims without evidence.

## Acquisition experiment

- **Google Search:** draft high-intent keyword groups around youth basketball stat tracking and player season stats; use distinct landing copy for coaches versus families. Start only after a conversion path and measurement are ready.
- **Apple Ads:** explore App Store acquisition for the installed coach app after the product and listing are ready, rather than treating a PWA as an App Store ad destination.
- **Organic:** publish helpful, accurate demonstrations of scoring and player history before buying broad awareness traffic.
- **Measure:** landing visits → relevant CTA clicks → sign-ups → first completed game → repeat usage. Separate interest in scoring from interest in game film. Do not count a processed reel as proof that a full game recording survived.

## Release gate before media-led ads

Verify complete full-game capture, finalization, playback near the end, upload/recovery, and a second game on real devices. Verify Live independently if it is in the claim. Test weak network and interruptions. Price streaming only after measuring per-game costs. Until then, lead with capabilities that are demonstrable and keep the concept route development-only.

The prototype lives at `/market-lab` in the web development preview. It is intentionally absent from production builds.
