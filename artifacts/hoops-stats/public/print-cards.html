🖨 How to print front & back on a home printer
  1. Open File → Print (or Cmd+P / Ctrl+P)
  2. Set paper size to US Letter, orientation Portrait, margins None
  3. Print Page 1 (fronts) only first
  4. Take the sheet out, flip it short-edge (top stays top), put it back in the paper tray
  5. Print Page 2 (backs) only
  6. Cut along the crop marks — you'll have 8 double-sided cards

Tip: if the backs come out upside-down, try flipping the paper the other way and reprinting Page 2.
