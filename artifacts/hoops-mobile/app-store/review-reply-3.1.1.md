# App Review Reply — Subscription Access

Use only if App Review asks how to reach the subscription flow.

---

## Reply Text

Thank you for your review. In the submitted build, reviewers can reach the
complete in-app purchase flow without using a web subscription or external
payment page:

1. Open the app and select **Sign in with Apple**.
2. A new free account is created.
3. Go to **Profile → Unlock Pro Features**.
4. Select Monthly or Annual and tap **Start Free Trial**.

The purchase is handled exclusively by Apple’s StoreKit purchase sheet. The
paywall also provides **Restore purchases**. After an App Store purchase,
Profile routes subscription management to Apple Account Settings.

The review notes include these steps and the submitted IAP product identifiers.
