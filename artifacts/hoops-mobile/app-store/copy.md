## Description (4000 chars max)
StecStats is the coaching tool that replaces the clipboard, the spreadsheet, and the dedicated camera — all in one app.

**Live Stat Tracking**
Track points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, fouls, and turnovers in real time during a game. Tap a player, tap a stat — that's it. The scorekeeper is built to keep up with the pace of a real game.

**Video Recording + Highlights**
Record your games directly in the app. StecStats automatically generates highlight and lowlight reels from your recorded footage so you can review key plays without scrubbing through an entire game.

**Career Stats**
Every game builds a player's career record. See shooting efficiency, totals, and trends across an entire season or roster from a single screen.

**Game Film Review**
After a game, watch the footage alongside the box score. Drill into individual player performance and share clips with your team.

**YouTube Live Streaming**
Go live to YouTube directly from the app. Parents and fans can watch from anywhere while you coach.

**Roster Management**
Build your roster once. Add players, rename them, or adjust your lineup between seasons — your history follows automatically.

**Free to start. Pro and Premium for serious coaches.**
The free tier covers full stat tracking for a single team. Upgrade to Pro for video recording and multiple teams, or Premium for live streaming, advanced film tools, and priority support.

---

Whether you coach youth rec ball or varsity, StecStats keeps the focus on the game — not the paperwork.
